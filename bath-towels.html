Bath and towel buying center

Practical guidance for choosing bath towels, hand towels, washcloths and coordinated sets.

Choose the right towel size

Bath sheets provide more coverage than standard bath towels but require more storage and drying space. Hand towels and washcloths should suit the household’s laundering frequency and available hooks or rails.

Compare material and weight

Cotton type, yarn construction, pile, density and finishing influence feel. GSM can help compare weight within similar products, but it is not a standalone guarantee of absorbency or durability.

Balance softness and drying time

Thicker towels may feel substantial but can take longer to dry. In humid bathrooms, airflow and laundering frequency matter. Avoid unsupported performance claims and verify the merchant’s composition and care instructions.

Care checklist

  • Follow the sewn-in care label.
  • Wash new colors separately when directed.
  • Avoid overloading the washer.
  • Dry fully before folding.
  • Review return restrictions on opened textiles.
